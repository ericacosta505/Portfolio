ABOUT

I stay close to the whole product.

ENGINEERING IS PART OF THE PRODUCT CONVERSATION—NOT A HANDOFF AT THE END.

I’m a software engineer based in Austin. I work as a Senior Software Engineer at FoundryZero, a venture studio that originates, prototypes, and operates software products.

My work spans product definition, mobile and web applications, APIs, data systems, cloud infrastructure, release engineering, and the operational work that follows launch. I created FoundryZero’s website and led Mosaic from an early product thesis to a live iOS and Android app.

Before FoundryZero, I spent three years at iCode Austin, moving from computer science instructor into campus and technology leadership. Earlier, I helped build Lafellus, an AI plagiarism detection startup supported through ASU Venture Devils. I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from Arizona State University.

I’m most interested in 0→1 products, small teams, and hard problems where technical judgment and product judgment belong in the same room.
